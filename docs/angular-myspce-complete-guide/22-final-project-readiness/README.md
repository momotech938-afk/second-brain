# Module 22 — Final Project Readiness

You've read the notes and finished the labs. This module answers one question honestly: **are you ready to build the real Myspce frontend?** It also gives you the plan for doing it.

Back to [README](../README.md) · Previous: [Module 21 Labs](../21-myspce-practice-labs/README.md)

## How to use this module

1. Go through the [concept checklist](01-concept-checklist.md) **without looking at notes**. Mark each item ✅ (can explain and use), 🟡 (shaky), ❌ (can't yet).
2. Do the [practical assessments](02-practical-assessments.md) in a fresh branch, with a timer.
3. Solve the [debugging exercises](03-debugging-exercises.md). Diagnose first, then check the answer.
4. Read the [implementation plan](04-implementation-plan.md) and adjust it to your schedule.
5. Score yourself with the [self-assessment rubric](05-self-assessment-rubric.md). You're **ready to build** at "Ready to build" on at least **10 of 12** rows.
6. For every 🟡/❌ item, re-read the linked note and redo the related lab task. Then re-score.

**Time:** ~2 h reading + ~8 h assessment work.

## Ground rules

- Confirmed facts vs proposals: the plan uses the confirmed decisions and the planned API from [MYSPCE_PROJECT_CONTEXT.md](../MYSPCE_PROJECT_CONTEXT.md). Everything else is labelled *proposed*.
- The backend isn't built. Milestones that need it say so, and the mock backend from Labs 6–8 covers the gap.
- "Ready" means you can build each piece **without copying from the labs**, look things up in the official docs when needed, and explain your choices.

## Self-assessment checklist

- [ ] I completed the concept checklist with no more than 10 🟡 and 0 ❌ items.
- [ ] I passed at least 5 of the 6 practical assessments within the time limit.
- [ ] I diagnosed at least 12 of the 15 debugging exercises before reading the answer.
- [ ] I adapted the implementation plan to my calendar.
- [ ] I scored "Ready to build" on at least 10 of 12 rubric rows.
- [ ] I updated [PROGRESS_TRACKER.md](../PROGRESS_TRACKER.md).
